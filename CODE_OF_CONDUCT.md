# Code of conduct

OrgRec contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation
in the project respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected conduct

- use welcoming and inclusive language;
- respect different experience, disciplines, identities, and viewpoints;
- give and accept specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- protect private research data and do not pressure anyone to disclose it;
- acknowledge sources, collaborators, and the limits of a claim;
- focus disagreement on the work, not the person.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information,
personal attacks, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a good-faith
reporter.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents OrgRec in
public project activities. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue
warnings, temporarily limit participation, or permanently exclude a person in
proportion to the conduct and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private
contact route published in the public GitHub repository. If that route is not
available, use GitHub's platform reporting tools. Do not put sensitive details
in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as circumstances
permit; conflicts of interest must be disclosed and routed to another reviewer
when possible.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Its attribution and
FAQ are available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/>.
